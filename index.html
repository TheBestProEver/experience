Hearth hear Earth

A jukebox that turns light into sound. Listen to NASA's Earth Information Center, to five vital signs of the planet from real measurements, and to the sky beyond it. Headphones recommended.

Press Enter to begin. The top row of the keyboard chooses a scene, E opens the Earth Information Center, Space pauses, N narrates, D speaks the data, L plays the legend, V switches the voice off and on, and question mark lists every key.

How the sound is made

Nothing here is a recording. Every scene is synthesised live in your browser from numbers: the pixels of an Earth Information Center frame, a temperature record, a wavelength, an orbit. Light is turned into pitch by dividing its frequency by 240, which lowers it forty octaves into hearing range without changing the ratios between colours; slower vibrations, like the ringing of the Sun or of Earth's electromagnetic cavity, are raised by a whole number of octaves instead. On that scaffold a small set of instruments — long bowed pads, undamped bells, plucked strings, filtered noise, a low brass swell and a Shepard tone — is driven by the data.

Each scene explains its own mapping under the live measurements, and the Legend button plays each sound on its own with its meaning, the way a map has a key. The climate records are read by script from NASA GISS, NOAA and NSIDC, with the source and a checksum kept for each. Imagery fetched live is provided by NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).

Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, The Earth Information Jukebox. Open source; runs entirely in the browser; works offline once loaded.

About

Hearth was built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, The Earth Information Jukebox. It is designed to be used without sight: everything is reachable from the keyboard, every scene can describe itself and its data aloud, and every number you hear is a real measurement. The address bar remembers the scene, source and preferences, so a link opens exactly where you were.
